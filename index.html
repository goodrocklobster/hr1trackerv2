About the data

Source: Connecticut DSS monthly assistance data. Counts cover 169 municipalities and may include the same person in multiple assistance categories. Counts below five are published as zero.

Net loss compares the selected month with July 2025; percent loss divides that difference by the July count. Negative changes are gains. July 2025 overlaps H.R.1’s enactment and is not a fully pre-law baseline. The figures do not establish how many people lost benefits because of the law.

District figures are estimates using supplied 2020 Census population shares and 2026 state legislative / 120th Congress district geography. The same shares are applied to every month; these are not historical boundary changes or recipient addresses. Rounded town shares are normalized to sum to 100%. Two unmatched geographic-code rows per file are excluded. Census boundary source.

H.R.1 context: expanded SNAP work rules began in November 2025; HUSKY D work rules begin January 2027. DSS policy timeline.
